Abstract: Submitted originally as a final project in CTS*1000, the article examines how the underrepresentation and sexualized design of female characters in video games contribute to real-world gender-based discrimination. Further focusing on the dicomfort and hostility these portrayals can foster within online spaces such as the gaming community.
